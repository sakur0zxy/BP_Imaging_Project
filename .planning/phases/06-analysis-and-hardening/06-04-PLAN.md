overview panel 的指标卡片建议显示：`status`、`recovery status`、`peak`、`PSLR`、`ISLR`、`IRW`。缺少点目标分析时显示 `pointTarget: disabled/skipped/missing`。
</target_visual_design>

- `recoveryEvaluation.cases.(caseName).artifacts.analysisResult.pointTarget.upsampled.image`
- `recoveryEvaluation.cases.(caseName).artifacts.analysisResult.pointTarget.xProfile.profileDb`
- `recoveryEvaluation.cases.(caseName).artifacts.analysisResult.pointTarget.yProfile.profileDb`
- `recoveryEvaluation.cases.(caseName).artifacts.analysisResult.pointTarget.xProfile.metrics`
- `recoveryEvaluation.cases.(caseName).artifacts.analysisResult.pointTarget.yProfile.metrics`

字段缺失时必须优雅降级：对应图块显示 `disabled / skipped / missing pointTarget`，不能报错中断整个评估。
</data_contract>
